MGSA's operational escalation pathways define who is notified, when, and through what channel for each category of operational issue. Pathways are tiered by severity and type: technology incidents follow the ITSM priority framework; compliance and regulatory issues route through the IO and Legal; vendor disputes route through Commercial and Legal; Board items are presented at the quarterly Technology and Risk Committee. All pathways are documented in the MGSA Escalation & Governance Framework (EGF-2024-001).
